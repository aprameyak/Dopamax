# Privacy Policy for Dopamax

**Last updated:** August 2025

## Introduction

Dopamax ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
Dopamax does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database.

### App Data
- Task information (title, completion status, creation date)
- Progress tracking data
- App preferences and settings

All this data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with any third parties.

## How We Use Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely for:
- Displaying your tasks and progress
- Tracking completion status
- Providing app functionality

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No cloud synchronization is performed
- Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

## Third-Party Services

Dopamax does not integrate with any third-party services that would collect your personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can:
- Delete individual tasks
- Clear all data by uninstalling the app
- Export your data (if you manually access the SQLite database)

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as the app is installed on your device. When you uninstall the app, all data is automatically deleted.
